Frequently Asked Questions

How does ImagePro Studio process files without uploading to a server?

ImagePro Studio executes all operations directly within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and pdf-lib. Zero bytes of your files or documents ever leave your computer or smartphone.

Can I resize photos and signatures for UPSC, SSC, NEET, and JEE forms?

Yes! ImagePro Studio includes one-click presets for competitive exams and government recruitment portals (UPSC, SSC, NEET, JEE, GATE, IBPS, State PSCs, Universities). You can crop and resize passport photos to exact 35×45mm dimensions, compress signatures to 10–20KB, and shrink PDF marksheets under 200KB or 100KB with 100% portal acceptance.

What image and document formats are supported?

For images, ImagePro supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, SVG, BMP, TIFF, and GIF format conversions and compression. For documents, it provides comprehensive editing, annotating, signing, watermarking, compressing, and two-way conversion between PDF and Microsoft Word formats (.docx, .doc, .docm, .dot, .dotx, .dotm).

How do I convert PDF to Word DOCX without uploading files?

Launch the 'PDF to Word' tool, drag and drop your PDF, and select your preferred output format (.docx, .doc, .docm, .dot, .dotx, .dotm). The in-browser engine parses text runs, reconstructs paragraphs, identifies headings (H1–H3), formats lists, and builds a native Word document directly in your browser with zero server contact.

How do I convert Word (.docx, .doc) files to PDF in-browser?

Open the 'Word to PDF' tool, upload your .docx or .doc file, adjust optional layout settings (A4 or US Letter, margins, font family, line spacing, and page numbers), and click 'Convert to PDF'. True vector text and images are typeset into a downloadable PDF that stays crisp at any zoom level.

Is ImagePro Studio completely free? Are there file limits or subscriptions?

ImagePro Studio is 100% free and unlimited forever. There are no subscriptions, account registrations, credit cards, watermarks added to your outputs, or daily file limits.
